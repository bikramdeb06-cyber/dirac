BuildMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. The app itself

The BuildMate app has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you enter in the app (project details, quantities, rates, tasks, contacts, notes) is stored only on your device. The app does not send this data to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Optional paid services (outside the app)

BuildMate links to optional professional services: building permission assistance and structural engineering. These services are provided outside the app. If you choose to request one, you will be taken to a web form or payment page in your browser. We may collect through that page:

  • your name, phone number and email address;
  • project details you choose to share (location, plot size, drawings, documents);
  • payment confirmation details from our payment provider. We do not see or store your full card or UPI credentials.

3. How we use this information

We use it only to respond to your request, provide the service, process payment, keep records required by law, and contact you about your request.

4. Sharing with outside engineers

If you ask us to connect you with an independent structural engineer or other professional, we share your name, contact details and the project information needed for the engagement with that professional, and only with your request. Independent professionals are responsible for their own handling of your data.

5. Other sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with our payment provider and form or hosting provider to run the service, and where the law requires.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep service records for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal and accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete your information by emailing us, except where we must keep it by law.

7. Children

BuildMate and its services are for adults and professionals and are not directed at children.

8. Changes

Updates will be posted at this address with a new date.
